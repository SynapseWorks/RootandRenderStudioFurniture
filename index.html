Beyond furniture

Builds that move, wear, perform, or tell a story.

These directions are part of the growing workshop rather than a promise that every technique is already mastered. The point is to learn by making increasingly ambitious physical things.

01

Props & practical effects

Handheld artifacts, set pieces, creature details, masks, scenic objects, and physical storytelling tools.

02

Cosplay fabrication

Foam, fabric, structure, finishing, wearable components, creature builds, and costumes designed to survive actual movement.

03

Mechanisms

Hinges, linkages, puppetry, moving features, simple electronics, lighting, servos, and practical motion built into an object or costume.

04

Animatronic experiments

Character and creature builds where fabrication meets motion, control systems, performance, and the wonderfully unnecessary ambition to make the thing actually move.

About the studio

Built slowly, with wonder.

Root & Render Objects is where restoration and fabrication meet. Furniture can be rescued and rewritten; props, costumes, mechanisms, and animatronics can be built from nothing; and any of them can become part of a larger story.
