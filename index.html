Craft and invention

Different materials. One workshop.

Restoration, props, costuming and experimental technology share a common practice: design something, test it honestly and make a better version.

01

Props & practical effects

Handheld artifacts, set pieces, creature details, masks, scenic objects, and physical storytelling tools.

02

Cosplay fabrication

Foam, fabric, structure, finishing, wearable components, creature builds, and costumes designed to survive actual movement.

03

Mechanisms

Hinges, linkages, puppetry, moving features, simple electronics, lighting, servos, and practical motion built into an object or costume.

04

Creative technology

Wearables, expressive mechanisms, robots and mobile prototypes—currently as studies or work in progress, never presented as finished inventions.

About the studio

Made with care and curiosity.

Root & Render Objects is where restoration and fabrication meet. From a rescued cabinet to a hand-carved fantasy staff or an early creature mechanism, the work begins with a question: what could this become? The archive distinguishes completed pieces, active experiments and ideas still waiting for their first prototype.
